# Modernity Party
## Code of Conduct

### Our Commitment

Modernity Party is committed to providing an open, respectful and constructive environment for everyone participating in the project.

We welcome people regardless of nationality, ethnicity, gender, age, disability, religion, personal background, political affiliation or socioeconomic status.

Participation is based on mutual respect and the willingness to engage with ideas.

## 1. Expected Behaviour

All participants are expected to:

- Treat others with dignity and respect.
- Criticise ideas rather than attack individuals.
- Engage in good-faith discussion.
- Respect the right to disagree.
- Distinguish factual claims from personal opinions.
- Provide sources for substantial factual assertions.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct misinformation.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Accept reasonable moderation decisions.

## 2. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following conduct is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation or threats.
- Discriminatory abuse.
- Targeted personal attacks.
- Deliberate publication of private information.
- Impersonation or fraudulent representation.
- Manipulation of participation or voting procedures.
- Deliberate dissemination of fabricated evidence.
- Persistent disruption of constructive discussion.
- Attempts to silence legitimate disagreement through intimidation.

## 3. Freedom of Expression

Modernity Party supports freedom of expression and open political debate.

Disagreement, criticism of leadership, criticism of adopted policies and alternative political proposals are legitimate contributions.

Moderation shall not be used merely to suppress unpopular opinions or criticism.

## 4. Political Pluralism

Participants may hold different political, philosophical and cultural positions.

No participant is required to agree with every proposal.

The project expects respect for democratic principles, human dignity and fundamental rights.

## 5. Moderation

Moderators should act:

- Consistently.
- Proportionately.
- Transparently.
- Without personal favouritism.
- With appropriate regard for context.

Where possible, participants should receive an explanation of moderation decisions and a reasonable opportunity to appeal.

## 6. Enforcement

Depending on the seriousness and repetition of the conduct, measures may include:

1. Informal clarification.
2. Formal warning.
3. Temporary restriction of participation.
4. Removal of inappropriate content.
5. Suspension or exclusion from project spaces.

Serious threats, harassment or unlawful conduct may require immediate action.

## 7. Reporting

Conduct concerns should be reported privately to the designated project moderators or maintainers.

Reports should include relevant information without unnecessarily distributing private data.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## 8. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub repositories.
- Project discussions.
- Official online communication channels.
- Project meetings and events.
- Other spaces where individuals are officially representing Modernity Party.

## 9. Accountability

Moderators and project leadership are equally subject to this Code.

No position within the project grants immunity from its standards.

---

**Our principle:**

Strong ideas do not require disrespectful behaviour.

Open debate requires mutual dignity.
