# Modernity Party
## Project Roadmap

**Modernitätspartei · MP**

**Version 0.1 — Initial Roadmap**  
**Status: Proposed**

## Purpose

This roadmap describes the intended development of Modernity Party as an open political project.

It establishes priorities, development stages and practical objectives. It is not a binding political programme, electoral commitment or guarantee of completion.

The project should develop through documented work, public contributions and democratic decisions.

## Phase 1 — Establish the Foundation

**Objective:** Create a clear, accessible and transparent project structure.

Tasks:

- [ ] Establish the public project repository.
- [ ] Publish the README and Founding Principles.
- [ ] Publish the Political Programme.
- [ ] Publish Governance principles.
- [ ] Publish Contribution Guidelines.
- [ ] Publish the Code of Conduct.
- [ ] Establish licensing and attribution rules.
- [ ] Create German and English documentation.
- [ ] Define how proposals, discussions and decisions are documented.

**Completion criteria:** A new contributor can understand the project, its principles, its current status and how to participate.

## Phase 2 — Develop the Political Programme

**Objective:** Translate the founding principles into concrete, assessable policy proposals.

Initial policy areas:

1. Democracy and constitutional institutions
2. Government and public administration
3. Justice and rule of law
4. Education and research
5. Economy and innovation
6. Science and technology
7. Healthcare and social development
8. Environment and sustainability
9. Infrastructure and mobility
10. Security and resilience
11. Europe and international cooperation

Tasks:

- [ ] Establish a separate directory for each policy area.
- [ ] Identify major challenges and unanswered questions.
- [ ] Collect reliable research and primary sources.
- [ ] Develop specific proposals.
- [ ] Assess legal, economic and social consequences.
- [ ] Identify implementation requirements and potential costs.
- [ ] Define measurable outcomes.
- [ ] Invite public criticism and alternative proposals.

**Completion criteria:** Each policy area has documented issues, research and proposals that can be evaluated independently.

## Phase 3 — Build Open Participation

**Objective:** Make political development accessible beyond a small founding group.

Tasks:

- [ ] Establish public discussion channels.
- [ ] Define transparent proposal and review procedures.
- [ ] Establish contributor roles and responsibilities.
- [ ] Develop translation and accessibility practices.
- [ ] Document disagreements and alternative proposals.
- [ ] Develop procedures for formal democratic decisions.
- [ ] Distinguish project participation from formal party membership.

**Completion criteria:** Contributors can participate meaningfully, understand how proposals are evaluated and identify who has authority to make formal decisions.

## Phase 4 — Develop Digital Infrastructure

**Objective:** Use technology to support transparency, participation and accountability.

Potential components:

- Public policy repository
- Searchable research archive
- Proposal and amendment tracker
- Public decision register
- Contributor directory with voluntary participation
- Translation management
- Accessible discussion platform
- Public financial reporting
- Institutional and administrative data analysis

Digital systems must respect privacy, security, accessibility and applicable law.

Technology should support democratic institutions, not replace democratic legitimacy.

**Completion criteria:** Digital infrastructure provides practical benefits and operates under transparent governance.

## Phase 5 — Establish Organisational Structures

**Objective:** Determine whether and how the project should develop into one or more formally constituted political organisations.

Tasks:

- [ ] Examine applicable national party and association laws.
- [ ] Define the relationship between the international project and national organisations.
- [ ] Develop draft statutes where appropriate.
- [ ] Establish democratic membership procedures.
- [ ] Define election and accountability mechanisms.
- [ ] Establish financial controls and reporting.
- [ ] Determine the requirements for formal registration and electoral participation.

Any national organisation must comply with the laws applicable in its jurisdiction.

**Completion criteria:** Organisational structures are established transparently and have the necessary democratic and legal foundations.

## Phase 6 — Continuous Evaluation

**Objective:** Ensure that the project remains capable of learning and improving.

Tasks:

- [ ] Review policy proposals against available evidence.
- [ ] Evaluate implemented policies and their outcomes.
- [ ] Publish progress reports.
- [ ] Review organisational effectiveness.
- [ ] Invite independent criticism.
- [ ] Revise outdated positions.
- [ ] Document lessons learned.

The roadmap itself should be reviewed periodically.

## Development principles

Throughout every phase, the project commits to:

- Transparency over opacity.
- Evidence over unsupported assertion.
- Democratic legitimacy over informal authority.
- Accountability over unreviewable power.
- Practical results over political appearances.
- International cooperation over unnecessary isolation.
- Continuous improvement over ideological rigidity.

## Roadmap governance

This document is a proposal.

Changes should be discussed openly and recorded in the repository. Priorities may be adjusted as the project develops.

A completed task does not automatically constitute formal political adoption of the resulting policy or organisational proposal.

**The roadmap is a direction, not a substitute for democratic decision-making.**
