# Modernity Party
## Governance and Democratic Organisation
### Version 0.1 — Governance Draft

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the proposed governance principles of Modernity Party.

The organisation shall be democratic, transparent, accountable, accessible and open to continuous improvement.

No individual, including the founder, shall possess unlimited authority over the organisation.

## 2. Organisational Principles

- Democratic legitimacy.
- Equality of membership rights.
- Transparency of decisions.
- Separation of responsibilities.
- Accountability of elected representatives.
- Protection of minority opinions.
- Documented decision-making.
- Regular review of organisational structures.

## 3. Membership

Membership shall be voluntary and governed by published membership rules.

Members should have:

- Access to the adopted programme and statutes.
- The right to participate in internal discussions.
- The right to propose amendments.
- Equal voting rights, subject to applicable law and valid organisational rules.
- Access to relevant organisational decisions.
- The right to leave the organisation.

Membership eligibility and formal membership requirements shall be established in accordance with the applicable national legal framework.

Supporters, contributors and registered website users are not automatically members.

## 4. Democratic Decision-Making

The organisation shall establish procedures for:

- Electing its executive leadership.
- Adopting and amending its programme.
- Approving organisational statutes.
- Reviewing financial reports.
- Establishing working groups.
- Resolving internal disputes.
- Removing officeholders through lawful democratic procedures.

Major decisions should be documented and published, subject to legitimate privacy and security restrictions.

## 5. Executive Organisation

The executive committee shall be democratically elected and accountable to the membership.

Its responsibilities should include:

- Implementing adopted decisions.
- Coordinating organisational activities.
- Managing resources.
- Representing the organisation.
- Maintaining compliance with applicable law.
- Publishing periodic activity reports.

Terms, election procedures, eligibility and removal mechanisms shall be specified in the formal statutes.

## 6. Working Groups

Working groups may be established for individual policy domains.

Examples:

- Democracy and Government.
- Science and Technology.
- Economy and Innovation.
- Education.
- Justice and Fundamental Rights.
- Health and Social Development.
- Environment and Sustainability.
- International Cooperation.

Working groups may research and propose policies but cannot independently declare proposals to be adopted party positions.

## 7. Open Development

The project shall use public version control for its philosophical and programme documents.

The public repository should provide:

- Revision history.
- Public issue tracking.
- Contribution guidelines.
- Recorded programme amendments.
- Clearly identified document status.
- Publicly accessible meeting summaries where appropriate.

Formal political decisions must follow the organisation's legally valid democratic procedures. GitHub approvals, stars, forks and issue reactions do not constitute party votes.

## 8. Financial Transparency

The organisation shall maintain accurate financial records.

It shall publish appropriate financial reports and disclose funding in accordance with applicable law.

Financial management must include:

- Documented income.
- Documented expenditure.
- Internal controls.
- Appropriate approval procedures.
- Conflict-of-interest disclosures.
- Independent auditing or review where required.

## 9. Conflicts of Interest

Officeholders and representatives should disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Personal, professional or financial interests must not improperly influence organisational decisions.

## 10. Protection of Democratic Rights

Internal procedures must protect:

- Freedom of expression.
- The right to disagree.
- Fair hearing.
- Equal treatment.
- Protection against arbitrary disciplinary action.
- Access to transparent appeal procedures.

## 11. International Participation

Modernity Party welcomes international intellectual contributions and cooperation.

The legal membership, leadership and organisational requirements of any formally established national party must nevertheless comply with the law of its jurisdiction.

An international contributor community does not automatically constitute a legally recognised political party.

## 12. Amendment

This document may be amended through public discussion and formal democratic adoption.

Changes to legally binding organisational statutes must follow the applicable statutory procedures.
