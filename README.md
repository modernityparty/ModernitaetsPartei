# Modernity Party
### Modernitätspartei · MP

**An open, international project for modern political thought and practical democratic reform.**

Modernity Party is an open-source political project dedicated to developing ideas, policies and institutions for a changing world.

We believe political systems must evolve alongside human knowledge, technological capabilities and societal needs. Established practice deserves scrutiny, progress requires responsibility, and public authority must remain accountable.

Our purpose is not simply to preserve existing political structures or replace one ideology with another. It is to develop better answers to real problems through reason, evidence, open discussion and democratic participation.

## Our principles

Our work is guided by twelve founding principles:

1. Human dignity
2. Freedom and responsibility
3. Reason and knowledge
4. Democracy
5. Progress and innovation
6. Institutional renewal
7. Social responsibility
8. Sustainability
9. International cooperation
10. Open political development
11. Continuous improvement
12. Responsibility for the future

Read the full [Founding Principles](FOUNDING_PRINCIPLES.md).

## What we are developing

The project includes:

- **Political programme:** proposals for government, democracy, education, economy, science, technology, justice, healthcare, sustainability and international cooperation.
- **Institutional reform:** practical approaches to transparency, accountability and effective public administration.
- **Research:** evidence, sources, comparative analysis and policy evaluation.
- **Open governance:** transparent processes for discussion, contribution and democratic decision-making.
- **Technology:** tools and digital infrastructure that support accessible participation and public accountability.

The programme is a working document. Proposals are open to scrutiny, revision and improvement.

## How to participate

You do not need to be a formal party member to contribute to this project.

You can:

- discuss political and institutional questions;
- propose policy changes;
- contribute research and sources;
- review and improve existing proposals;
- help with software, documentation and translations;
- identify problems and suggest practical solutions.

Start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Participation in this repository does not automatically constitute formal party membership, a legally recognised vote or an official election-support signature.

## International by design

Modernity Party is not limited to Germany.

The project welcomes international perspectives, contributions and cooperation. Its initial documentation is maintained in German and English, with further translations encouraged.

Where a formal political organisation is established in a particular country, it must comply with that country's applicable laws. The international project and any legally constituted national party must be distinguished clearly.

## Democratic organisation

The project aims to develop transparent and accountable governance.

Its proposed organisational principles include equal participation rights, elected representatives, documented decisions, financial transparency and fair procedures for disagreement and appeal.

See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Project status

**Version 0.1 — Foundational development**

Modernity Party is currently an open political development project. Its founding principles, programme and governance proposals are drafts, not yet formally adopted party statutes or an officially established political party.

We will document substantive changes and distinguish proposals, working drafts and formally adopted decisions.

## The central idea

Modernity is not a label for a political identity. It is a responsibility: to question what no longer works, understand what can be improved, and build institutions that serve people today and future generations.

**Modernity is the responsibility to make tomorrow better than today, without forgetting the dignity of those living today.**
